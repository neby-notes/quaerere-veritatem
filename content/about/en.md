**Quaerere Veritatem** means, in Latin, "to seek the truth": an activity and its object, not a doctrine.

This site is a *digital garden*: independent essays, each studying a concept, an author, a tradition or a question, linked to one another by conceptual affinity. It is not organized by disciplines, because reality is one and disciplines are not cuts in it but instruments of the intellect that seeks it.

What is treated here is truth in its several registers — the propositional, the personal, the artistic —; the desire to know for its own sake and the examined life; the unity of knowledge; and intellectual work understood as contemplating and transmitting: one investigates in order to understand, and one publishes in order to hand on to others what has been understood.

## NEBY

I sign as **NEBY**. The name arose from acronyms and transformations of earlier names; only afterward did I notice its phonetic closeness to the Arabic *nabī* and the Hebrew *nāḇîʾ*, terms meaning "prophet". A resonance discovered a posteriori, not the origin of the name.
