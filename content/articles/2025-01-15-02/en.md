# Quotations and Voice Fragments

Extended quotations use Libre Baskerville to differentiate themselves from the body text. No additional classes are needed: the renderer decides automatically.

> Truth is a proposition that cannot be proven or refuted within the system in which it is stated. This is why the search for truth always exceeds the limits of any method.

A normal paragraph after the quote, to check vertical rhythm between elements.

> Great minds think alike. The minor details, however, are where authentic creativity resides.
>
> — attributed to Anonymous

Another transition paragraph that visually separates the quotations from the rest of the content.

> Every knowledge system rests on assumptions it cannot prove. Recognizing this is not a weakness, but the very condition of thought.
