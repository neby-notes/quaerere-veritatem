# Main Title (h1)

This is an introductory paragraph accompanying the main title. Notice how the size and line height adapt automatically to the hierarchy level.

## Second Level (h2)

The h2 introduces a new section. The top spacing is greater than the bottom, creating a clear vertical rhythm.

### Third Level (h3)

The h3 works as a subsection. From here the scale decreases in a controlled manner.

#### Fourth Level (h4)

This is an h4 within the same section. Its weight remains 600 but the size is smaller.

##### Fifth Level (h5)

The h5 is aligned with the base body size but with greater weight.

###### Sixth Level (h6)

The h6 is the deepest level, useful for marginal notes or internal references.
