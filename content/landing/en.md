title: "Quaerere Veritatem"
---

> "All men by nature desire to know" — Aristotle, *Metaphysics*, 980a21.

Quaerere Veritatem is a site that gathers and links concepts from different origins, ordered by the reality they seek to understand and not by the disciplines, in order to contemplate it and communicate it.
